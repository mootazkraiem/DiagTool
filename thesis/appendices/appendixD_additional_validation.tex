\section{Top alerting CAN identifiers}

The five most frequent identifiers in the post-correction alert list were \texttt{0x19}, \texttt{0x7EC}, \texttt{0x7E4}, \texttt{0x13}, and \texttt{0x7BB}. The three eights-prefixed identifiers (\texttt{0x7EC}, \texttt{0x7E4}, \texttt{0x7BB}) are consistent with UDS diagnostic request/response traffic, matching the recording's active-polling capture method described in Appendix~\ref{app:datasets}.
