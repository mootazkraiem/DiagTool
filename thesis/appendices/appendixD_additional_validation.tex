\section{Top alerting CAN identifiers}

Figure~\ref{fig:app-top-canids} ranks the eight most frequent identifiers in the post-correction alert list. The identifiers \texttt{0x7EC}, \texttt{0x7E4}, \texttt{0x7BB}, \texttt{0x7A8}, \texttt{0x7B3}, and \texttt{0x7A0} all fall in the \texttt{0x7A0}--\texttt{0x7EC} range reserved for \gls{uds} diagnostic request/response pairs, consistent with the recording's active-polling capture method described in Appendix~\ref{app:datasets}. Identifier \texttt{0x19}, the single most frequent alert source, and \texttt{0x13} are broadcast identifiers rather than diagnostic ones, and correspond to the high-frequency battery and drivetrain messages discussed in Section~\ref{sec:validation-rootcause}.
