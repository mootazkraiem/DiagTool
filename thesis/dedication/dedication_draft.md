To my dear family,

To my mother, whose strength was never loud, yet never failed me. Your patience, your sacrifices, and the quiet dignity with which you carried what others could not see have shaped more of me than words could ever account for. Whatever steadiness I possess, I owe in no small measure to you.

To my father, for supporting my studies and for playing a part in the road that led me here.

To my brothers, whose presence has remained a constant thread through the years: for the shared laughter, the disagreements, the silence, and the simple certainty that some bonds require no explanation.

To someone dear, who understood without demanding an explanation, who waited without complaint, and whose presence made difficult distances seem less so. Some debts are not measured in what is given, but in what is quietly endured beside us. This is for you.

And finally, to myself.

For having walked through doubt without allowing it to become a verdict, and for refusing to mistake a difficult chapter for the conclusion of the story.

I have been my own harshest judge. No longer.

The road ahead remains unwritten. Good. I have never required certainty to begin.

This is the first step of a promise I made to myself, one I intend to keep.
